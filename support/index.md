# ETLock support

ETLock is made by Hamish and Cameron McAlpine, two brothers who race. If something is wrong, or you
have an idea, or you just want to tell us what ET your car ran, write to us.

Email: support@etlock.app

A racer answers — usually Hamish or Cameron, often from the track — so give us a day or two if it is
race weekend.

### When you write, it helps to tell us

- What phone you have, and whether it is an iPhone or Android.
- The version number, from the About screen in the app.
- What you were doing, and what you expected.
