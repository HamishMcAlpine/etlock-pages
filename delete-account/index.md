# Delete your ETLock account

**App:** ETLock  
**Developer:** Hamish and Cameron McAlpine


You can delete your ETLock account and everything stored against it. It takes about half a minute
in the app, and it cannot be undone.

### In the app (the quick way, and it happens immediately)

1. Open ETLock.
2. Go to Settings.
3. Tap Account.
4. Tap "Delete my account". If you never added an email, the button reads "Delete everything on this
   phone and the server" — it does the same job.
5. Read the warning, then tap "Yes, delete everything".

That is it. Your account and everything stored against it are removed straight away, and the phone
forgets its own copies.

### If you can't open the app

Write to support@etlock.app from the email address you signed in with, and say you want your ETLock
account deleted. A racer reads that inbox — usually Hamish or Cameron. We will confirm it is you by
replying to that same address, then delete the account. We do it within 30 days, and we will tell you
when it is done.

If you never added an email to your account, it is an anonymous account: it has no name, email or
phone number on it, so we have no way to tell from the outside which one is yours. Open the app on the
phone you used and delete it there. If you have already removed the app, an anonymous account holds
no name, email or phone number.

### What gets deleted

- Your Cars, Runs and Race days.
- Your rain alerts and the notification address we kept to send them.
- The diagnostic trace linked to your account, if you ever turned that on.
- Any email, Apple ID or Google ID you linked to the account.
- The account itself.

### What we keep

Nothing about you or your account. The only records that remain are Apple's and Google's own
purchase records, if you ever bought anything: those belong to Apple and Google and they keep them
under their own terms. Crash reports never carried your account ID, so there is nothing in them to
match to you.

### How long it takes

Immediately, if you delete in the app. Within 30 days, if you ask by email.
